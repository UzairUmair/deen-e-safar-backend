# Illustrated Deen AI Academy

An editorial cream hero introduces seven learning tracks with distinct original artwork. Track cards show actual catalog counts and beginner level. Search, filters, continue cards, review status and original study library remain functional. Selecting a card lesson opens a preview with illustration, objectives, source limitations, outline and Start Lesson. Existing direct/resume links continue into the workspace.

The workspace retains explicit previous/save/continue controls, real session restoration, source cards and footnotes, private notes and server-scored quizzes. The outline collapses on mobile; the AI tutor is a separate expandable panel. Explanation actions, bounded follow-up context, English/Urdu/Roman Urdu, evidence validation, review revocation and authenticated quotas reuse the academy backend. No historical passage is approved by artwork or by model output.

Images are pre-generated WebP files served locally through unoptimized next/image (compatible with static export), with reserved dimensions, lazy card loading, object-fit crops and visible illustration labels. The hero is 16:9, cards 4:3 and lesson headers crop to 3:2. Shared AnimatedButton and reduced-motion behavior remain.

There are 13 original assets: seven unique track environments, a desert-route hero/Hijrah environment, and dedicated Uhud, Khandaq, Hudaybiyyah, Makkah and Yusuf environments. Remaining lessons use their relevant track's thematic artwork. They do not yet have unique per-lesson commissions; these are not presented as specific event reconstructions or Companion portraits. Historical biographies remain awaiting review as documented in the source policy.
