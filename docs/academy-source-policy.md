# Academy source and image policy

The existing policy in ai-academy-source-policy.md remains authoritative. Only approved and license-approved SourcePassage records may ground generated lessons. Exact existing Quran/Hadith text, attribution, translation footnotes and grading are retained. Historical outlines are editorial plans until approved passages are mapped to their steps. New illustrations supply no historical evidence and never change review status.

No Prophet, named Companion portrait, invented dialogue, sacred Arabic, historical date or religious ruling is generated by the illustration system. All 13 selected images contain empty environments or objects. Labels explicitly say they are AI illustrations, not historical evidence. Historical appearance and topography are inspired environments, not verified reconstructions. Qualified review of historical content, edition rights, disputed reports and semantic entailment is still required before expanding the curriculum.

The model's citation checks validate source identity and current approval; they do not establish that every generated claim follows from the passage. No live academy Groq output or new production OAuth flow is claimed as tested in this local redesign.
