## Historical corpus shortlist

The strongest machine-readable research candidate found is
[OpenITI](https://openiti.org/documentation/#how-can-i-use-openiti-what-license-is-used),
an academic corpus with identifiable text versions. Its published corpus license
is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: attribution, noncommercial and share-alike conditions matter.
This is **conditional eligibility**, not approval for a commercial app or every
edition/translation. Its documentation describes OCR/edition issues and removal
of copyrighted editorial material. A cleaned text or academic host does not
authenticate every report. Pin a release/commit, inspect metadata, collate selected
passages against the printed edition, and arrange historical/isnad review.

## Permission-first textbook candidate

[The Sealed Nectar / Ar-Raheeq al-Makhtum, Darussalam product page](https://darussalamstore.com/products/the-sealed-nectar-ar-raheeq-al-makhtoum)
identifies Safiur Rahman Mubarakpuri's Seerah work. The inspected product page did
not provide an open reuse license. Treat it as permission-required: obtain written
rights for the exact edition/language, storage, excerpts, AI grounding and any
translations before ingestion. A publicly reachable PDF elsewhere is not evidence
of permission. Publisher provenance also does not replace passage-level review.

## Acquisition and scholarly review checklist

For each selected passage record source ID/type, title, author, edition, exact
volume/page or section, language, source URL, authenticity/context note and
lesson/step assignments. Keep a rights record with the grant or license URL,
attribution wording, use restrictions and review date. Use the existing pending
ingestion and explicit admin review; neither research candidates nor AI output
can grant themselves approval.

Begin with Quran/Hadith-backed passage studies. Pilot a few carefully collated
Seerah/Sahaba/event passages only after rights and scholarly review. Include
questions about Badr/Uhud, conflicting reports, unsupported exact dates and
fabricated dialogue in a human-reviewed evaluation set. Mark uncovered steps as
awaiting review. No historical corpus is needed to keep improving tutor code.
